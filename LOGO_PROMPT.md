# Logo Design Prompt for W-AI

## Company Overview
**Name**: W-AI  
**Tagline**: WhatsApp Marketing & Automation Platform for E-commerce  
**Industry**: SaaS, Marketing Automation, AI-Powered Communication

## Core Brand Identity

### Brand Values
- **Intelligence**: AI-powered automation and smart responses
- **Connection**: Seamless WhatsApp communication
- **Efficiency**: Streamlined marketing workflows
- **Innovation**: Cutting-edge technology for e-commerce
- **Trust**: Reliable and secure platform

### Target Audience
- E-commerce business owners
- Marketing professionals
- Online retailers (especially Salla platform users)
- Businesses looking to automate customer communication

## Logo Design Requirements

### Style & Aesthetic
- **Modern & Clean**: Minimalist design that conveys professionalism
- **Tech-forward**: Should feel innovative and AI-powered
- **Approachable**: Friendly and accessible, not intimidating
- **Scalable**: Works well at small sizes (favicon) and large sizes (billboards)
- **Versatile**: Works in both light and dark backgrounds

### Visual Elements to Consider
1. **WhatsApp Connection**: Subtle reference to messaging/communication
   - Chat bubbles, message icons, or connection lines
   - Avoid direct WhatsApp logo to prevent trademark issues

2. **AI Intelligence**: Represent artificial intelligence
   - Neural network patterns, circuit-like connections
   - Sparkles, stars, or light rays suggesting intelligence
   - Abstract geometric shapes suggesting data processing

3. **E-commerce/Marketing**: Business and growth elements
   - Upward arrows, growth charts
   - Shopping cart or product references (subtle)
   - Connection nodes representing networks

4. **Letter "W" Integration**: 
   - Creative integration of the letter "W" into the design
   - Could be formed by negative space, geometric shapes, or abstract forms

### Color Palette - W-AI Brand Colors
**Primary Color Scheme (Forest Green Theme):**
- **Primary**: `#1D4F34` - Forest Green (main brand color)
- **Primary Light**: `#2D6A4F` - Medium green
- **Primary Lighter**: `#40916C` - Bright green
- **Primary Lightest**: `#52B788` - Light green
- **Accent**: `#B4D4C0` - Soft mint green (accent/highlight)

**Supporting Colors:**
- **Info**: `#4A90D9` - Blue (for informational elements)
- **Warning**: `#FF6B35` - Orange (for alerts/notifications)
- **Success**: `#1D4F34` - Forest Green (same as primary)
- **Background**: `#F6F7F7` - Light gray (light mode)
- **Foreground**: `#1A1D1F` - Dark gray (text)
- **Card**: `#FFFFFF` - White (surfaces)

**Gradient Options:**
- Primary gradient: `#1D4F34` → `#40916C` → `#52B788`
- Full gradient: `#1D4F34` → `#2D6A4F` → `#40916C` → `#52B788` → `#B4D4C0`

**Dark Mode Colors:**
- Primary: `#40916C` (brighter green for dark backgrounds)
- Background: `#0F1419` - Very dark blue-gray
- Card: `#16202A` - Dark blue-gray

**Quick Color Reference:**
```

```

### Typography Considerations
- **Font Family**: Tajawal (Arabic-friendly, modern sans-serif)
- **Style**: Clean, readable, tech-forward
- **Geometric**: Suggests precision and structure
- **Rounded**: Friendly and approachable (matches UI border radius of 12px)
- **Bold**: Confident and strong presence

### Design System Alignment
- **UI System**: shadcn/ui (New York style)
- **Border Radius**: 12px (0.75rem) - modern rounded aesthetic
- **Icon Library**: Lucide icons
- **Design Philosophy**: Clean, modern, professional with subtle rounded corners

## Technical Specifications

### Formats Needed
- **SVG**: Vector format for web and scaling
- **PNG**: High resolution (1024x1024px minimum) with transparent background
- **Favicon**: 32x32px and 16x16px versions
- **Monochrome**: Black and white versions for single-color applications

### Use Cases
- Website header/favicon
- Mobile app icon
- Social media profiles
- Marketing materials
- Email signatures
- Business cards
- Presentation slides

## Additional Notes

### What to Avoid
- Direct use of WhatsApp logo or trademarked elements
- Overly complex designs that don't scale well
- Cliché AI imagery (robot heads, etc.)
- Generic tech company aesthetics
- Hard-to-read fonts at small sizes

### Inspiration Keywords
- Modern SaaS logos (Stripe, Notion, Linear)
- Communication platforms (Slack, Discord)
- AI/ML companies (OpenAI, Anthropic)
- Marketing automation tools (HubSpot, Mailchimp)
